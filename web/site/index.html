hlsimii: Highline Fall Simulator II

WARNING: This tool is only for experimenting - it does not replace evaluating highline spots with experienced riggers.

Documentation
Spot

Anchors sit at (0, left height) and (L, right height): L is the horizontal distance between them.

Tension

Set one of the two: the webbing is pulled at the chosen side, either by a fixed length or until the unloaded line reaches the standing tension.

Note: as in the ISA calculator, the height column mixes points: for "Standing" it is the lowest point of the unloaded line (its sag), for "Walking" it is the leash ring on the line, for the falls it is the slackliner's harness (ring minus leash length), at its lowest point. Anchor and leash tensions of the falls are the simulator's peaks (checked every integration step). "Settled" is the static equilibrium of the broken line with the slackliner hanging in the leash.
